# prog-metal
<h1>The Beginner's Guide to Modern Progressive Metal</h1><br>
<h3>INTRODUCTION</h3>
Welcome back, guys. Enclosed in this guide is a six part series introducing you to the genre of progressive metal. If you’d like to see the full flow chart, you can download the full .pdf <a href="https://www.example.com">here.</a> This guide was created to help those who are new to the genre of modern progressive metal, and maybe even metal in general, get started without being either overwhelmed or just not being able to find something that appeals to their taste. My approach to this guide is to help steer you in the right direction but also give you the different paths you can take to go down the rabbit hole.

The beauty of progressive metal is just how far the genre expands. It’s a broad genre with very undefined parameters, so we’re going to need to hone the interests of the listener (that’s you) so we can find what your tastes are. We’re starting off with six very different bands and moving down but also outwards, branching out into other genres of metal and rock. If you don’t like what you’re hearing, skip down to the next bullet. And if you like everything you hear, well, just keep going. Just because you don’t like the first band doesn’t mean you’ll hate all the ones in the rest of the category, so try a few before completely moving on to the next section.

WARNING: I’ve broken these bands down into categories based purely on sound. That’s the only parameter. This means instrumentation and vocal styles, not including things like lyrical content or general themes, and especially not who influenced who. This is a sonic progression through the genre, not a progression through the bands that influenced each other. For example, I have Tool as a subcategory to Opeth and Soen. I understand that Soen has been influenced by Tool, but I’m creating this list in a way that benefits the listener and caters to sound, taking careful steps into the genre rather than just shoving the most influential bands at you.

Rather than being a definitive guide, think of this as taking a quick cursory glance at the different variations of progressive metal, and picking what sticks out to you the most so you can take that further on your own journey.

Now let’s get started.
